One Student. One Phone. One Chance.  •  OFFLINE DEMO

Made for a real classroom, not a smart classroom

No college WiFi needed. No projector needed. No new machine to buy. Just the teacher phone and student phones you already have, simple words, big buttons, and everything offline. That is why users will actually use it.

No internet needed after this file opens. All attendance is saved on this phone only, in the browser's offline storage. Demo checks: one QR per class + Room 304 tick + phone owner. This file already opens on Android, iOS, and laptop browsers. The full version will be one cross-platform app, not Android only. The full version adds phone GPS (works without college WiFi or internet) and phone biometric confirmation (fingerprint, no face photo stored) plus a random second check in class. No college WiFi, no projector, and no extra machine to buy are needed.

Class report — teacher's phone

In a real classroom, student phones connect to the teacher phone's offline hotspot (no internet). The same live count then updates on the teacher's phone. This demo simulates that on one phone, so you can show the full flow to a teacher.

Demo day timetable. Replace these lines with your real class timetable. Break 11:00-11:15 and lunch 1:15-2:00 match your class pattern; the subject order here is a demo example.



Real notifications: at class start, both teacher and student get “NOW IS YOUR SE class from 9 am to 10 am”. At class end, both get “You have 5 more minutes to mark your SE attendance” and “Now from 10 am to 11 am, it is your CN class”. After those 5 minutes, that class attendance closes automatically.

Progress — attendance percentage

In the full version, a student sees only his own progress. The teacher sees the whole class. Progress counts only closed classes, not the live session.

Message to teacher — private

Your identity is NOT saved. The teacher sees only the message, not who wrote it, not your phone, and not your name. Write freely. This is for students who hesitate to speak directly, and for CRs to pass class information.
