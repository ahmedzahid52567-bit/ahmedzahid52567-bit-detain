# DETAIN — 5 to 8 Minute Pitch Script
Prepared by Zahid Ahmed
Tagline: One Student. One Phone. One Chance.
Practice at normal speed: about 7 minutes. Do not rush. Pauses are part of the aura.

---

## [0:00 - 0:45] THE HOOK — I WAS THERE, BUT I WAS ABSENT

Good morning everyone.

One day, I was told I was absent.
But I was present.
I was sitting right there, on that broken wooden bench, in Room 304, listening to the class.

[Pause. Look at the audience.]

My name was not on the sheet. My attendance was short. And suddenly, I was not allowed to write my internal test.

I did not come here today to complain.
I came here to build.
Because when a system can erase a student who was sitting right in front of you, that system does not need an excuse. It needs a replacement.

That replacement is DETAIN.

## [0:45 - 2:00] THE STORY — WHY I BUILT THIS

Let me be honest with you.
I am not a topper from a smart classroom.
My college does not have strong WiFi. Our projector works sometimes, and sometimes it does not. Our benches are broken. Our classrooms are simple.

But I attended. Every day, I attended.
And still, one sheet, one mark, one shortage, could decide my future.

[Pause.]

That day I understood something.
Attendance in our colleges is not proof. It is memory. It is a paper. It is a guess.
And a guess can be wrong. A guess can be forwarded. A guess can be faked.

A friend can take a photo of a QR code and send it to a friend sitting in the hostel. That friend marks proxy, and no one knows.
A student who was present has no proof. A teacher who worked honestly also has no proof.

So I asked myself one question:
What if attendance was not a mark, but proof?
Proof that this student, on this phone, was in this room, at this class time.

That question became DETAIN.

## [2:00 - 3:00] THE NAME — I KEPT IT WANTEDLY

Now, why DETAIN? I kept this name wantedly, on purpose.

DETAIN means Digital Education Tracking Attendance Identification Network.
But detain also means to hold. To stop something from escaping.

[Slow, strong.]

DETAIN holds the truth, so it cannot escape.
DETAIN detains proxy, so it cannot escape.
DETAIN detains doubt, so it cannot escape.
DETAIN detains unfair shortage, so it cannot decide a student's future again.

I did not choose a soft name. I did not choose a cute name.
I chose a name that does its job, the moment you hear it.

One Student. One Phone. One Chance.

## [3:00 - 4:30] HOW IT WORKS — SIMPLE, OFFLINE, REAL CLASSROOM

DETAIN is simple, because our classroom is simple.

Teacher comes to class. One tap on her phone. One QR, one code, for the whole class. Not one QR per student. Not a new QR every 15 seconds. One. That is it.

No projector? No problem. Teacher says the code aloud, or writes it on the board. Students enter that code.

No college WiFi? No problem. DETAIN works offline. It uses phone GPS, not college WiFi. It uses the phones we already have. No new machine to buy. No smart board. No biometric machine.

Student scans, or enters the code. Then three checks pass together: Are you in this room? Is this your phone? Is this your fingerprint?
The phone only says yes or no. No face photo is stored. No photo database. Your fingerprint stays in your phone, where it already is.

And the teacher phone shows it live: Properly marked, 42 out of 60. Not a guess. A live count.

At 10 o'clock, the system says: You have 5 more minutes to mark SE attendance. Now from 10 to 11, it is CN class.
After 5 minutes, SE closes. The sheet locks.

[Show the demo here, if you have it.]

## [4:30 - 5:30] THE DEMO — WATCH IT, DON'T JUST HEAR IT

Let me show you, not just tell you.

Teacher starts the session. See the QR. See the code.
Student marks. See the count rise. See the receipt. Time, room, subject, receipt code. That receipt is the student's proof. If anyone doubts later, the receipt speaks.

Now watch the proxy test.
Arjun tries to mark for Rohan.
[Pause.]

What happens? Rohan gets no mark.
And Arjun? Arjun also loses his own attendance for this class.

This is my Double Loss Rule.
Try proxy, lose your own.

A blurry scan is only a retry. A failed scan is only a retry. But a confirmed proxy attempt? You lose your own chance. That is fair to everyone. Fair to the student who came. Fair to the teacher who taught. Fair to the class.

And no public shaming. The class sees only numbers. The flagged name goes privately to the teacher only.

Teacher closes the session. The sheet locks. No silent edit. If there is a correction, it stays visible, with a reason. No one can quietly change history after the class.

## [5:30 - 6:30] MORE THAN ATTENDANCE — THE FULL SYSTEM

DETAIN is not only attendance. Because a classroom is not only attendance.

Student gets his progress. Green, Yellow, Red. He knows where he stands, before it is too late. No surprise shortage at exam time.

A hesitant student, who is afraid to speak to a teacher, can send a private message. Teacher sees only the category, the message, and the time. No name. No phone. No device. That is privacy, by design.

And teacher also has her own updates box. Book checking tomorrow. Record correction date. Class test on Friday. Assignment details. One place, for the whole class. No chasing students one by one.

Timetable notifications. Progress. Private messages. Teacher updates. All offline. All simple. All on Android, iOS, and laptop. One app, for everyone.

## [6:30 - 7:30] WHY THIS MATTERS — FOR BOTH SIDES

I am not here to fight my teacher.
I am here to fix the system that failed both of us.

My teacher also deserves proof. Proof that she took the class. Proof that she did honest work. Proof that no one can doubt later.

A student deserves proof. Proof that he was present. Proof that his future will not be decided by a missing mark.

DETAIN protects both sides. That is why a teacher can accept it. That is why a student can trust it.

I built this from a broken bench, with no WiFi, with no projector, with no money for a new machine. I built it free, offline, and simple, because that is the real India. That is the real classroom.

If it works in my classroom, it will work in any classroom.

## [7:30 - 8:00] THE CLOSE — AURA LINE

So, let me end where I started.

One day, I was told I was absent.
Today, I am standing here, present, with proof.

They said shortage.
I built DETAIN.

[Pause. Slow. Look up.]

My name is Zahid Ahmed.
This is DETAIN.
One Student. One Phone. One Chance.
No proxy. No favour. No doubt.

Thank you.

## DELIVERY NOTES — READ THIS BEFORE YOU PRESENT

- Do not name any teacher in the script. Say "my teacher" only. That keeps your aura strong and your case clean.
- Do not rush the pauses. The pauses after "I was absent" and after "Try proxy, lose your own" are where the audience feels it.
- Practice 3 times with a timer. If you go over 8 minutes, cut the Progress / Messages section short, never cut the Hook, the Name, or the Double Loss Rule.
- If a judge asks "Is this unique?", do not say "No one ever did QR attendance." Say: "QR alone is not unique. My combination is: one QR per class, plus room, phone, biometric, live count, Double Loss Rule, receipt, and locked sheet, all offline and simple."
- If a judge asks "What if a student has no phone?", say: "Desk verified. Teacher checks ID face to face at the desk, saved separately as Desk verified, not as a normal scan."
